J10

Flanges and webs with concentrated forces

AISC 360 Section J10: the six local limit states of a wide-flange member under a concentrated force. Each figure builds the member, its load path and its load, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

06

Flange local bending

J10.1 · A plate welded across the flange pulls on it; the flange bends about the web and yield lines form.

    07

    Web local yielding

    J10.2 · The reaction at a bearing support spreads through the flange at 2.5 : 1 and yields the web at the fillet toe over N + 5k.

      08

      Web local crippling

      J10.3 · Over a bearing support the web wrinkles just above the flange, and the beam settles into it.

        09

        Web sidesway buckling

        J10.4 · With the flanges free to move sideways, the loaded flange swings out and the web bends under it.

          10

          Web compression buckling

          J10.5 · Equal loads on both flanges at one section bow the web out across its depth.

            11

            Web panel-zone shear

            J10.6 · An unbalanced beam moment shears the column web between the continuity plates.
