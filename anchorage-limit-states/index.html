Anchorage to concrete

ACI 318 Chapter 17: the ways a cast-in or adhesive anchor in concrete fails, in tension and in shear. Each figure casts the block, sets the anchor, loads it, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

43

Steel tension rupture

The rod necks just above the concrete and breaks, and its top comes away with the load.

    44

    Concrete breakout in tension

    ACI’s idealized prism: concrete out to 1.5 hef from the anchor every way, its faces sloping 1 : 1.5 down to the head, pulls out with it. ANco = 9 hef².

      45

      Pullout

      The head crushes the concrete above it, and the anchor slides up out of the block without a cone.

        46

        Side-face blowout

        A deep anchor close to an edge bursts the side face out at the level of its head, 3 ca1 each way, the width ACI gives one anchor.

          47

          Bond failure

          Adhesive anchor · The bond along the rod lets go, and the rod, its adhesive and a shallow cone at the surface come out together, inside ACI’s influence square 2cNa on a side.

            48

            Steel shear rupture

            The rod shears off where the plate meets the concrete; the plate and the top of the rod slide, drawn hidden through the plate.

              49

              Concrete breakout in shear

              ACI’s idealized half pyramid: between the anchor and the free edge, 1.5 ca1 each way along it and 1.5 ca1 down its face, the concrete breaks out toward the edge. AVco = 4.5 ca1².

                50

                Pryout

                A short, stiff anchor turns under shear, and its embedded end pries out the half of its tension breakout prism behind it; ACI bases Vcp on that breakout.
