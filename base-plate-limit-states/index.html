Base plate limit states

A column base plate on a concrete pier, AISC Design Guide 1 and ACI 318 Chapter 17. Each figure loops on its own; move across a drawing to scrub it, or pick a stage under it.

DG1

Column base plate

AISC Design Guide 1: a W12×65 column on a 22 in square, 1½ in plate, grouted on a 32 in square concrete pier and held by four cast-in 1 in rods. The concrete in bearing (AISC 360 J8), the plate in bending at its bearing and tension interfaces, the column welds, a large moment, and the rods and the pier in tension and shear (ACI 318 Chapter 17). Each figure casts the pier with its rods, sets the plate and column, loads it, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

59

Concrete bearing

Under the column’s load the grout and the concrete under the plate crush, and the pier splits outward from under it. AISC 360 J8 caps the bearing stress at 0.85 f′c √(A2/A1).

    60

    Plate yielding in compression

    The bearing pressure bends the plate’s cantilevers, m beyond 0.95d and n beyond 0.8bf, up off the grout about yield lines round the column.

      61

      Plate yielding in tension

      The column pulls up and the rods hold the plate down; the plate bends between them about lines at the flanges and at the rods.

        62

        Anchor rod rupture

        Under uplift the rods neck just above the grout and break, and the plate and column lift away.

          63

          Pier breakout in tension

          The rods sit 7½ in from the pier’s sides, inside 1.5 hef, so ACI’s prism takes the whole top of the pier: it cracks round the sides and lifts out with the column.

            64

            Column weld rupture

            The fillet welds round the column’s foot tear through their throats and the column lifts off the plate.

              65

              Moment and uplift

              A large moment rocks the column and plate about the toe; the heel lifts and its rods stretch, and the grout under the toe takes the bearing.

                66

                Anchor rod shear

                The plate slides on the grout and the rods, bent through the grout, shear off at the concrete. ACI takes 0.8 of their strength on a grout pad.

                  67

                  Pier breakout in shear

                  Pushed toward the near side, the front rods, 7½ in from it, break out a wedge of the pier, across its whole width and 1.5 ca1 down its face.
