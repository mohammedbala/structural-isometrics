    Written for Mechanical APDL with the SOLID65 concrete element. Recent releases list SOLID65 as a legacy element; the current alternative pairs SOLID185 with the Drucker–Prager concrete model (TB,CONCR,,,,DP) or the microplane model (TB,MPLANE), and the geometry, meshing and load steps stay the same. Deflections, crack patterns and convergence graphs in the animation are hand-calculation estimates and sketches, not solver output.
