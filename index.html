Structural isometrics

Five structural models drawn in lines, four in concrete and one bolted steel connection, then the local limit states of wide-flange members under concentrated forces of rectangular and round HSS-to-HSS connections, of the end-plate moment connection, and of the shear tab. Each builds stage by stage in a loop. Pick colours below and download any figure as SVG, PNG or GIF. Move across a drawing to scrub it, or pick a stage under it. Deflections, cracks and mode shapes are sketches, not analysis results.

            06

            Flange local bending

            J10.1 · A plate welded across the flange pulls on it; the flange bends about the web and yield lines form.

              07

              Web local yielding

              J10.2 · The reaction at a bearing support spreads through the flange at 2.5 : 1 and yields the web at the fillet toe over N + 5k.

                08

                Web local crippling

                J10.3 · Over a bearing support the web wrinkles just above the flange, and the beam settles into it.

                  09

                  J10.4 · With the flanges free to move sideways, the loaded flange swings out and the web bends under it.

                    10

                      11

                        K

                        Rectangular HSS-to-HSS connections

                        12

                        Chord wall plastification

                        T-connection, β ≤ 0.85 · The branch pushes the connecting face in, and the face folds along yield lines from the footprint out to the sidewalls.

                          13

                          Punching shear

                          Shear yielding, 0.85 < β ≤ 1 − 1/γ · The face shears around the footprint of a wide branch, and the branch pushes the plug into the chord.

                            14

                            Sidewall local yielding

                            β = 1 · A branch as wide as the chord bears straight onto the sidewalls, and they yield over 5k + lb just below the corners.

                              15

                              Sidewall local crippling

                              β = 1, T-connection in compression · The sidewalls wrinkle just under the loaded face, and the branch settles into them.

                                16

                                Sidewall local buckling

                                β = 1, cross-connection · Branches push on both faces at one section, and the sidewalls buckle out across the depth: the cross-connection case of sidewall crippling.

                                  17

                                  Branch local yielding

                                  Uneven load distribution, β > 0.85 · The stiff sidewalls draw the load into the branch walls above them and an effective width of the cross walls, which yield at the weld.

                                    18

                                    Chord sidewall shear

                                    Gapped K-connection · One branch pushes and one pulls, and their vertical components shear the chord sidewalls across the gap.

                                      19

                                      Chord distortional failure

                                      T-connection, out-of-plane moment · The branch moment racks the chord section into a parallelogram under it.

                                        K

                                        Round HSS-to-HSS connections

                                        AISC 360 Chapter K, tube to tube: round HSS branches welded to a round HSS chord along saddle-shaped intersections. Chord plastification governs T-, cross- and K-connections, and punching shear is checked when the branch is nearly as wide as the chord. Displacements are exaggerated.

                                        20

                                        Chord plastification, T

                                        T-connection, β = 0.6 · The branch pushes the chord wall in; the wall dents around the saddle and the section ovalizes.

                                          21

                                          Chord plastification, cross

                                          Cross-connection · Branches push on both sides at one section, and the chord squashes into an oval between them.

                                            22

                                            Chord plastification, K

                                            Gapped K-connection · The pushed branch dents the chord wall in and the pulled one draws it out, either side of the gap.

                                              23

                                              Punching shear, round

                                              Shear yielding, β = 0.8 · A branch nearly as wide as the chord shears the wall around its saddle and pushes the plug in.

                                                358

                                                End-plate connection limit states

                                                AISC 358 Chapter 6: the limit states of the four-bolt extended end-plate (4E) moment connection, the plate, bolts and weld first, then the column. Each figure builds the connection, bolts it and loads the beam, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

                                                24

                                                End-plate yielding

                                                Thin-plate behaviour · The plate bends round the tension flange along yield lines at the flange and the bolt rows, while the bolts hold.

                                                  25

                                                  Bolt tension rupture

                                                  Thick-plate behaviour, no prying · The plate stays flat and turns with the beam about its compression flange; the four tension bolts stretch and break.

                                                    26

                                                    Extended plate shear

                                                    Shear yielding, then rupture · The outer bolts hold the extension while the flange pulls the rest of the plate away, shearing it across its thickness at the flange.

                                                      27

                                                      Bolt shear rupture

                                                      The beam shear goes to the bolts at the compression flange; the plate slides down on them and they shear at the faying surface.

                                                        28

                                                        Plate bearing and tear-out

                                                        Heads left off · The beam shear slides the plate down past the bolts; its holes stretch into slots above them, and the plate tears out between the lower holes.

                                                          29

                                                          Flange weld rupture

                                                          The weld of the tension flange tears, the flange pulls off the plate, and the web weld unzips from the top.

                                                            30

                                                            Column flange yielding

                                                            No continuity plates · The bolts pull the column flange out; it bends about the column web and its tips curl toward the beam.

                                                              31

                                                              Column flange bearing and tear-out

                                                              Seen from behind the column, nuts left off · The bolts ride down with the plate and bear on the column flange; its holes stretch and it tears out between the lower holes.

                                                                32

                                                                Column web yielding

                                                                No continuity plates · The tension flange force spreads through the plate and the column flange and yields the web at the toe of its fillet over 6k + tbf + 2tp.

                                                                  33

                                                                  Column web crippling

                                                                  No continuity plates · The compression flange pushes the plate into the column, and the web wrinkles just behind the column flange.

                                                                    34

                                                                    Panel-zone shear

                                                                    The flange forces shear the column web between the continuity plates into a parallelogram; the column flanges kink and the beam turns with it.

                                                                      10

                                                                      Shear tab limit states

                                                                      AISC Manual Part 10: the limit states of a conventional single-plate shear connection, a ⅜ in tab welded to the column flange and bolted to the beam web with four ¾ in bolts. Each figure builds the connection, bolts it and loads the beam, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

                                                                      35

                                                                      Bolt shear

                                                                      The eccentric reaction shears the bolt group; the beam drops and turns about the group’s instantaneous centre, and the bolts shear where the tab meets the web, drawn hidden.

                                                                        36

                                                                        Tab bearing and tear-out

                                                                        Heads left off · The web carries the bolts down; the tab’s holes stretch below them, and the tab tears out to the next hole and from the lowest hole to its bottom edge.

                                                                          37

                                                                          Web bearing and tear-out

                                                                          Tab behind the web, nuts left off · The beam drops past the bolts; the web’s holes stretch above them and the web tears out up to the hole above.

                                                                            38

                                                                            Tab shear yielding

                                                                            The tab yields in shear on its gross section between the weld and the bolts, and the bolt line drops with the beam.

                                                                              39

                                                                              Tab shear rupture

                                                                              The tab tears along the line of bolts, through the holes, and the strip outside it drops with the beam.

                                                                                40

                                                                                Tab block shear

                                                                                A block of the tab outside the bolts and below the top bolt tears out: shear down the bolt line, tension across to the free edge.

                                                                                  41

                                                                                  Weld rupture

                                                                                  The fillet welds tear, and the tab slides down the column flange with the beam.

                                                                                    42

                                                                                    Web block shear

                                                                                    Coped beam, tab behind the web, nuts left off · The corner of the web inside the bolts stays on them as the beam drops: shear up the bolt line to the cope, tension across to the beam’s end.
