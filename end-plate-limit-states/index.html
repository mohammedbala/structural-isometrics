End-plate connection limit states

AISC 358 Chapter 6: the limit states of the four-bolt extended end-plate (4E) moment connection, the plate, bolts and weld first, then the column. Each figure builds the connection, bolts it and loads the beam, then lets the mechanism develop. Displacements are exaggerated.

24

End-plate yielding

Thin-plate behaviour · The plate bends round the tension flange along yield lines at the flange and the bolt rows, while the bolts hold.

    25

    Bolt tension rupture

    Thick-plate behaviour, no prying · The plate stays flat and turns with the beam about its compression flange; the four tension bolts stretch and break.

      26

      Extended plate shear

      Shear yielding, then rupture · The outer bolts hold the extension while the flange pulls the rest of the plate away, shearing it across its thickness at the flange.

        27

        Bolt shear rupture

        The beam shear goes to the bolts at the compression flange; the plate slides down on them and they shear at the faying surface.

          28

          Plate bearing and tear-out

          Heads left off · The beam shear slides the plate down past the bolts; its holes stretch into slots above them, and the plate tears out between the lower holes.

            29

            Flange weld rupture

            The weld of the tension flange tears, the flange pulls off the plate, and the web weld unzips from the top.

              30

              Column flange yielding

              No continuity plates · The bolts pull the column flange out; it bends about the column web and its tips curl toward the beam.

                31

                Column flange bearing and tear-out

                Seen from behind the column, nuts left off · The bolts ride down with the plate and bear on the column flange; its holes stretch and it tears out between the lower holes.

                  32

                  Column web yielding

                  No continuity plates · The tension flange force spreads through the plate and the column flange and yields the web at the toe of its fillet over 6k + tbf + 2tp.

                    33

                    Column web crippling

                    No continuity plates · The compression flange pushes the plate into the column, and the web wrinkles just behind the column flange.

                      34

                      Panel-zone shear

                      The flange forces shear the column web between the continuity plates into a parallelogram; the column flanges kink and the beam turns with it.
