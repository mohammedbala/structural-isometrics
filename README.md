# structural-isometrics

Animated, isometric line studies of structural models: four in concrete, one bolted steel connection, the six AISC 360 J10 flange and web limit states for wide-flange members, the AISC 360 Chapter K limit states for rectangular and round HSS-to-HSS (tube to tube) connections, and the AISC 358 limit states of the extended end-plate moment connection. Each page is one looping black-and-white animation; move across the drawing to scrub it, or pick a stage from the rail underneath.

**Everything on one page:** [`index.html`](index.html), served by GitHub Pages at https://mohammedbala.github.io/structural-isometrics/ once Pages is turned on (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main`, `/ (root)`).

On that page you can recolour every figure (presets such as ink, night, blueprint, paper, phosphor and redline, or your own background and line colours) and download any figure as SVG or PNG (the frame on screen, optionally with a transparent background) or as an animated GIF of one full loop. The GIF encoder is [gifenc](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc) by Matt DesLauriers (MIT), inlined.

The folders below hold each animation on its own page.

- [`concrete-hairline/index.html`](concrete-hairline/index.html): a concrete beam, built in eleven stages: dimensions, keypoints, lines, area, volume, partition, materials, mesh, supports, loads and cracking.
- [`turbine-foundation/index.html`](turbine-foundation/index.html): a tabletop turbine-generator foundation, built in ten stages: column grid, keypoints, frame, raft plan, volumes (raft, columns, beam-grid table top), materials, mesh, soil springs, the machine with its unbalance, and the first two vibration modes.
- [`pump-foundation/index.html`](pump-foundation/index.html): a pump pedestal foundation, built in eleven stages: outline, keypoints, lines, footing area, volumes (footing, cast-in anchor bolts, pedestal pour), grout and baseplate with nuts, materials, mesh, piles, the pump and motor running, and two vibration modes (rocking and torsion).
- [`building-substructure/index.html`](building-substructure/index.html): the substructure of an industrial building, built in five stages against a fixed grade datum: slabs (outlines at four floor levels: hall +1′-0″, dock +4′-0″, office +1′-6″, pit −3′-0″), walls (room walls, then pit walls), slab on grade (hall one foot above grade, then dock and office, then pit), materials, and mesh. No footings. Heights are drawn at an exaggerated scale.
- [`moment-end-plate/index.html`](moment-end-plate/index.html): an AISC 358 four-bolt extended end-plate moment connection (4E), built in ten stages: work lines, a W14×132 column, a W24×76 beam, the 1¼ in end plate, flange and web welds, holes, eight 1⅛ in A490 bolts and nuts, column continuity plates, turn-of-nut pretension, mesh, and a moment that rotates the beam so the plate separates from the column flange at the tension side.
- [`wide-flange-limit-states/index.html`](wide-flange-limit-states/index.html): the six AISC 360 Section J10 limit states for flanges and webs with concentrated forces, one looping figure each: flange local bending (J10.1), web local yielding (J10.2), web local crippling (J10.3), web sidesway buckling (J10.4), web compression buckling (J10.5) and web panel-zone shear (J10.6). Each builds the member, its load path and load, then the mechanism. Displacements are exaggerated sketches, not analysis output.
- [`hss-limit-states/index.html`](hss-limit-states/index.html): the AISC 360 Chapter K limit states for HSS-to-HSS (tube to tube) connections, one looping figure each. Each branch is an open tube with its axial force drawn as an arrow. Rectangular branches on an HSS 10 × 10 × ½ chord: chord wall plastification, punching shear (shear yielding of the connecting face), sidewall local yielding, sidewall local crippling, sidewall local buckling in a cross-connection, local yielding of the branch from uneven load distribution, shear of the chord sidewalls in the gap of a K-connection, and chord distortional failure under an out-of-plane moment. Round branches on an HSS 10.000 × 0.500 chord, meeting it along saddle-shaped intersections: chord plastification in T-, cross- and gapped K-connections, and punching shear. Each builds the chord, the branch and its load, then the mechanism. Displacements are exaggerated sketches, not analysis output.
- [`end-plate-limit-states/index.html`](end-plate-limit-states/index.html): the AISC 358 Chapter 6 limit states of the four-bolt extended end-plate (4E) moment connection, one looping figure each. Beam side: end-plate flexural yielding (thin-plate yield lines), bolt tension rupture (thick plate, no prying), shear yielding and rupture of the extended part of the plate, bolt shear rupture at the compression flange, bolt bearing and tear-out at the plate, and rupture of the tension flange weld. Column side: column flange flexural yielding, bolt bearing and tear-out at the column flange, column web local yielding over 6k + tbf + 2tp, column web crippling, and panel-zone shear. The column-web figures and the column-flange bearing figure are seen from behind the column, where the web next to the bolted flange is in view; the bearing figures leave the bolt heads or nuts off so the holes show. Displacements are exaggerated sketches, not analysis output.

All are drawn on the [hairline](https://github.com/lucasmarkes/hairline) kernel by Lucas Marques (MIT), inlined unchanged. The pages are written as artifact bodies (no `<html>`/`<head>` wrapper); opened directly in a browser they still render. Deflections, crack patterns and mode shapes are illustrative sketches, not analysis output.
